const std::uint32_t p_numBodyMutexes = 0,
const std::uint32_t p_maxBodyPairs = 1024,
const std::uint32_t p_maxContactConstraints = 1024 )

Initializes the Jolt job and physics systems.

Parameters
p_maxBodiesMaximum number of bodies to support.
p_numBodyMutexesNumber of body mutexes to use. Should be a power of 2 in the range [1, 64], use 0 to auto detect.
p_maxBodyPairsMaximum amount of body pairs to process (anything else will fall through the world), this number should generally be much higher than the max amount of contact points as there will be lots of bodies close that are not actually touching.
p_maxContactConstraintsMaximum amount of contact constraints to process (anything else will fall through the world).
